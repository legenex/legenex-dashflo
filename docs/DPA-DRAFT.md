# DashFlo Data Processing Addendum

Status: internal review draft, not approved for signature or publication

This draft is intentionally incomplete. It must not be offered to a customer
until every review marker is resolved by the owner and legal counsel.

## Parties and scope

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements the agreement between Next
Consulting LLC dba DashFlo ("DashFlo") and the customer identified in the
applicable order form ("Customer"). It applies where DashFlo processes personal
data on Customer's behalf in providing the DashFlo services.

The parties acknowledge that their legal roles depend on the data, purpose,
instructions, and applicable law. Where Customer determines the purposes and
means of processing Customer Data, Customer acts as controller or business and
DashFlo acts as processor, service provider, or contractor. Each party remains
responsible for processing it performs for its own purposes.

## Processing details

- Subject matter: lead intake, validation, suppression, routing, delivery,
  reporting, billing operations, portals, integrations, support, and security.
- Duration: the applicable service term plus the period needed for authorized
  return or deletion, backup expiration, audit, dispute, security, and legal
  obligations.
- People: Customer personnel; suppliers; buyers; business contacts; and people
  represented in lead and call data.
- Data: account and contact information; lead and call content; accident,
  injury, treatment, insurance, and related information; consent and attribution
  records; commercial and financial information; device, log, audit, delivery,
  and integration data; communications; and Customer-provided content.
- Operations: collection, receipt, storage, organization, normalization,
  validation, deduplication, suppression, retrieval, consultation, analysis,
  transmission, delivery, reporting, archiving, deletion, and other processing
  documented by Customer.

## Customer instructions and responsibilities

DashFlo will process Customer Data on documented instructions, including the
agreement, configured workflows, authorized user actions, and support requests,
unless law requires otherwise. Customer is responsible for lawful instructions,
notices, consents, data minimization, recipient selection, marketing compliance,
and responding to data-subject requests as controller or business.

DashFlo will notify Customer if it believes an instruction violates applicable
data-protection law, unless prohibited from doing so.

## Confidentiality and access

DashFlo will limit access to personnel and providers who need it for authorized
processing and who are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.
Customer will configure roles and integrations consistently with least-access
principles and protect credentials and endpoints under its control.

## Security

DashFlo will maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational
measures appropriate to the processing risk.

REVIEW REQUIRED: attach an approved security schedule covering, at minimum,
access control, authentication, credential management, encryption decisions,
logging, vulnerability management, incident response, backup and recovery,
change control, tenant and portal isolation, and provider oversight. Do not
promise encryption or certification beyond verified production controls.

## Requests and assistance

Taking account of the nature of processing, DashFlo will reasonably assist
Customer with requests from individuals and with Customer's obligations for
security, incident response, impact assessments, and regulator consultation,
to the extent required by applicable law and available information. DashFlo may
refer a request to Customer when Customer controls the data.

## Security incidents

DashFlo will notify Customer without undue delay after confirming a breach of
Customer Data for which notice is required under applicable law. Notice will
include available information reasonably needed for Customer's response and
will be updated as material facts become known.

REVIEW REQUIRED: approve contractual notification time, notice recipients,
cooperation limits, and definition of a reportable incident.

## Subprocessors

Customer authorizes DashFlo to use subprocessors needed to provide the Services,
subject to written obligations appropriate to their processing. DashFlo remains
responsible for its subprocessor obligations to the extent required by law and
the agreement.

REVIEW REQUIRED: identify the active production provider legal entities,
processing locations, purposes, notice method, objection process, and replacement
procedure. Do not publish a guessed list based only on optional code.

## Return and deletion

At the end of Services, DashFlo will, at Customer's written choice and subject
to applicable law, return or delete Customer Data within a commercially
reasonable period. Deletion from backups may occur through the ordinary backup
cycle, with access restricted in the interim.

REVIEW REQUIRED: approve category-specific retention periods, export format,
backup cycle, legal-hold process, and treatment of immutable audit, consent,
suppression, delivery, and financial records.

## Audits and information

DashFlo will provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate the
obligations in this DPA. Any audit must protect other customers, security,
confidential information, and service availability.

REVIEW REQUIRED: choose a documentary review, questionnaire, independent report,
on-site audit, cost allocation, frequency, and notice framework.

## International transfers

No international-transfer mechanism is incorporated by this draft.

REVIEW REQUIRED: determine whether Customer Data is transferred from the EEA,
United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another jurisdiction requiring contractual
transfer terms. If needed, counsel must add the correct controller and processor
modules, appendices, competent authority, governing member state, UK Addendum,
and supplementary measures.

## Consumer health data

Where DashFlo processes consumer health data on Customer's behalf, Customer
will identify applicable law and provide instructions consistent with required
notice, consent, sale authorization, access, withdrawal, deletion, and appeal
rights. DashFlo will process such data consistently with this DPA and the public
Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

REVIEW REQUIRED: address Washington RCW 19.373, Nevada NRS 603A.400 to 603A.550,
and any other applicable health-data statute against actual geographic flows.

## Liability, conflict, and legal terms

The liability provisions of the main agreement apply to this DPA unless the
parties expressly agree otherwise. This DPA controls only for conflicts about
its subject matter.

REVIEW REQUIRED: legal name and notice address, effective date, order of
precedence, governing law, venue, signatures, and the relationship between DPA
liability and the main agreement.
